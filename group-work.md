# Group work

Work in teams of **maximum 3 students** and choose **one of two cases**: one in the public sector or one in the private sector. The project takes place during weeks 5–10.

## Goal

The goal is to be able to **defend your modeling choices**. Explain how your model represents the decision problem, why you chose its objective and constraints, and which assumptions you made. Discuss how those assumptions affect the results and the recommendation you would give to a decision-maker.

## Defending your modeling choices

Use these questions to guide your work:

- What decision needs to be made, and who is making it?
- What are your decision variables, objective, and constraints, and why are they appropriate?
- What assumptions and simplifications have you made, and what are their limitations?
- How do your results support your recommendation, and how could changes in the data or assumptions affect it?
